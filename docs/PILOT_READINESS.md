# Commercial pilot readiness

A real pilot is an external manual gate. Before enrolling a customer, complete signed clean installs, policy/legal/privacy approval, synthetic restore and crash rehearsals, an authorized supervised dry run, support staffing and data-deletion rehearsal.

Canary criteria: one reviewed low-risk synthetic rehearsal first; no unresolved S0/S1 defects; clean current-policy revalidation; encrypted backup verified; no reconciliation backlog; explicit operator authorization. A real canary must stop after one claim and the account outcome must be reconciled by an authorized human before any continuation.

Success metrics: installation completion, tracking/classification accuracy sampled by a human, `REVIEW_REQUIRED` rate, dry-run completion, uncertain/duplicate rate, time to reconcile, support incidents by severity, recoveries based only on recorded amounts, restore success and deletion completion. Never invent refund values.

Feedback template: customer role/platform/locale; workflow attempted; expected/actual result; severity; synthetic or authorized-data declaration; redacted evidence; workaround; consent for follow-up. Post-pilot review: metrics, incidents, policy variance, privacy requests, support load, go/no-go conditions, rollback and retained/deleted data.

Rollback: stop submission, preserve audit history, reconcile uncertainty, clear session if appropriate, restore the verified encrypted backup, validate integrity, and document every affected claim. Deletion: export if authorized, remove selected customer records/evidence and external copies, verify, then issue a completion record without exposing identifiers.
